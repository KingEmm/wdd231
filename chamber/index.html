Ohaozara is a prominent Local Government Area in Ebonyi State, Nigeria, known for its rich cultural heritage, significant salt deposits, and agriculture. Comprising three major towns—Uburu, Okposi, and Ugwulangwu—it is a, major economic hub in the region for farming (yam, cassava, rice) and artisanal salt production.
